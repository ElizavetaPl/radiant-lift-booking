# Radiant Glow Booking

Create a high-converting, fully responsive premium landing page and interactive consultation booking web application using React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons.

PROJECT OVERVIEW:

- Product/Service: "Безоперационный лифтинг и сияние" (Combined protocol: Ultrasound SMAS-lifting + Premium biorevitalization).

- Price: 45,000 RUB (Special promotional price instead of 60,000 RUB).

- USP: Rejuvenating the deep muscle-aponeurotic layer and enhancing skin quality in 1 visit, with zero downtime and zero "overfilled/frozen" face effect.

- Target Audience: Women aged 35–50, medium+ to high income, value natural aging gracefully, discretion, and time efficiency.

- Primary Conversion Goal: Booking a 3D-modeling online consultation with a cosmetologist or claiming a 50% discount on an initial in-person appointment.

DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETICS (PREMIUM LUXURY):

- Color Palette: Deep graphite / obsidian background (#1A1A1A and #242424), crisp light text (#F8F9FA), and muted champagne gold accents (#D4AF37) for interactive CTA elements, pricing badges, and subtle borders.

- Typography: Elegant serif headers paired with ultra-clean, legible sans-serif body text. Generous whitespace ("air") and card layouts with subtle borders.

- UI Components: Clean card grids, interactive accordion for FAQ, high-end testimonial blocks, and a multi-step modal or embedded lead capture form.

LANDING PAGE STRUCTURE & SECTIONS:

1. Header / Navigation: Minimalist sticky header with brand name, trust badge, and a "Записаться со скидкой" quick action button.

2. Hero Section: 

   - Powerful H1: "Безоперационный лифтинг и сияние кожи за 1 визит — без реабилитации и эффекта «перекачанного лица»"

   - Subheadline detailing the dual protocol (SMAS-lifting + biorevitalization).

   - Primary CTA button opening the booking form. Trust indicators (written contract, certified premium drugs).

3. Pain Points Section ("Узнаете себя?"):

   - 3 interactive cards highlighting client fears/pains: 

     1. Ptosis / sagging contours & jowls.

     2. Tired morphotype (dull complexion, deep nasolabial folds, fine wrinkles).

     3. Fear of unnatural "plastic surgery victim" look or frozen mimics.

4. Solution Section ("Как это работает"):

   - Interactive breakdown of the 2-step protocol combining deep SMAS hardware tightening and dermal hydration.

5. Benefits & Guarantees:

   - 4 key value pillars: Zero downtime, 100% certified authentic products opened on-site, official medical contract, preserved natural facial expression.

6. Social Proof / Testimonials:

   - Real patient review card with age, location, and emotional transformation story ("looked like I just came back from a great vacation").

7. Offer & Pricing Card:

   - Special promo display: 45,000 RUB (crossed out 60,000 RUB). Clear countdown or limited-slots urgency indicator.

8. Interactive Booking / Lead Form Modal:

   - Fields for Name, Phone, Preferred format (Online 3D-consultation vs. In-person clinic visit). Form validation with success state confirmation message.

9. FAQ Accordion: Addressing top objections regarding pain, safety, and recovery time.

10. Footer: Legal medical disclaimers, contact numbers, address placeholders, and privacy policy links.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

- Mobile-first layout optimized for 80% mobile traffic fluidity.

- Clean component architecture (modular React components inside src/components).

- Fully interactive forms with validation and smooth state transitions.
